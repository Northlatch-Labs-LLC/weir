2.1 **Subscriptions and unlocks.** When a supporter pays for your gated content, the Contract deducts a platform fee of 2.9% and credits the remainder to your creator vault in the same transaction. 2.2 **Tips.** A tip settles through the same Contract function as a subscription or unlock and is charged the same 2.9% platform fee. The remainder is credited to your creator vault alongside your other earnings and is withdrawn the same way. A tip buys the supporter nothing and expires never.

2.3 **Pooled yield.** If you open a vault, supporters may deposit SUI into it. The vault delegates that SUI to a validator. A platform fee of 2.9% is deducted from the staking yield and accrues to Northlatch; what remains is yours. You may set a share of your own remainder to be returned to your supporters, up to all of it — it is taken from your part, never from the platform's, and the figure is recorded on the vault where anyone can read it. **The deposited principal is not yours.** It remains the supporter's property, recorded to their address, withdrawable by them at any time, and no function in the Contract allows you or Northlatch to transfer it. You may not represent a pooled deposit as yours, as a donation, or as money you can spend.

2.4 **Yield is variable.** Staking yield depends on validator performance and network conditions. It may be zero for any period. Northlatch makes no representation about the yield your vault will produce.
