## 4. How money moves on Weir

Weir supports four kinds of transaction. Each is executed by the Contracts; Northlatch is not a party to any of them.

4.1 **Pooling (support vaults / "Treasury").** You may deposit SUI into a creator's support vault. The support vault delegates the SUI to a validator. Of the staking yield the delegation earns, a platform fee of 2.9% is deducted first and accrues to Northlatch; the remainder is the creator's, except that a creator may set a share of their own remainder to be returned to the people pooled behind them. Where a creator has set such a share, your part of it accrues in proportion to what you deposited and is claimable by you from the vault; Northlatch cannot withhold it and does not hold it. Your deposited principal is recorded to your address, is not transferred to the creator or to Northlatch, and may be withdrawn by you at any time, subject to the Sui network's staking withdrawal mechanics (withdrawals of staked SUI take effect at the next epoch boundary). The cost to you of pooling is the yield you forgo, less any share the creator has chosen to return. **Pooling is not a gift, loan, or investment in the creator, and confers no rights against the creator or Northlatch.**

4.2 **Subscriptions and unlocks.** You may purchase access to a creator's gated content for a period (a subscription) or to a single item (an unlock). Payment settles on chain in USDC or SUI. Access is represented by an object in your wallet and is enforced by Seal key-release against that object. A platform fee of 2.9% of the amount paid is deducted by the Contract at settlement and accrues to Northlatch; the remainder is credited to the creator's vault in the same transaction, where only the creator's own capability can withdraw it. Prices are set by creators. **All subscription and unlock payments are final and non-refundable**, except as required by law or as stated in Section 4.6.

4.3 **Tips ("Chests").** You may send SUI or USDC to a creator through the Contract. A tip is a voluntary gift: it buys nothing, grants no access, expires never, and is not refundable. It settles through the same Contract function as a subscription and is charged the same 2.9% platform fee; the remainder is credited to the creator's vault with their other earnings.

4.4 **Names.** You may register a `.sui` name through the Service. Names are issued by the SuiNS protocol, not by Northlatch, under SuiNS's own terms, and are delivered directly to your wallet in the same transaction as payment. The price shown includes the SuiNS registration fee and a Northlatch service fee of US$7.00 per registration, charged in SUI at the live rate and displayed before you confirm. The service fee is fixed at the moment you sign and is not charged on renewals unless you renew through the Service. Registering directly with SuiNS is always available to you and costs less; our fee pays for the interface and the single-transaction delivery. Northlatch does not hold, renew, or control any name after delivery. Renewals are your responsibility.

4.5 **Network fees.** Every transaction requires a Sui network fee. Where we sponsor that fee (for example on a first transaction), we may stop doing so at any time.

4.6 **Failed transactions.** If a transaction fails on chain, no value moves and no access is granted. If a transaction succeeds but content is later unavailable, Section 7.5 applies.

4.7 **No reversal.** Blockchain transactions are irreversible. We cannot reverse, refund, or recover any transaction, including one sent to the wrong address or for the wrong amount.
